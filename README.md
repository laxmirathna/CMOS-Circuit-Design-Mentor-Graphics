# CMOS Circuit Design and Transistor-Level Simulation Using Mentor Graphics EDA

## 1. Project Overview

This repository presents my practical work in CMOS circuit design, transistor-level implementation, and circuit simulation using Mentor Graphics Tanner EDA tools.

The primary objective of this work is to develop an understanding of CMOS technology through schematic-level circuit development and simulation. It involves working with NMOS and PMOS transistors, CMOS logic structures, digital circuit implementation, and semiconductor memory architectures.

Using Tanner S-Edit and T-Spice, I developed circuit schematics, prepared testbenches where applicable, performed simulations, and analysed circuit behaviour through simulation waveforms.

This work helped me connect theoretical concepts from semiconductor devices and digital electronics with practical circuit-level implementation.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Understand the working principles of CMOS technology and MOS transistors.
- Develop transistor-level CMOS circuit schematics.
- Understand the implementation of digital logic using NMOS and PMOS transistors.
- Study the electrical characteristics and switching behaviour of MOSFETs.
- Create testbenches for applicable circuit designs.
- Perform circuit simulations and analyse output waveforms.
- Explore CMOS-based memory structures and their operating principles.
- Gain practical familiarity with EDA design and simulation workflows.

## 4. Technical Concepts Covered

### CMOS Technology

CMOS (Complementary Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor) technology uses complementary NMOS and PMOS transistors to implement digital circuits.

The work explores complementary transistor operation, pull-up and pull-down network concepts, CMOS switching behaviour, logic-level representation, and transistor-level implementation of digital circuits.

### NMOS and PMOS Characteristics

Studied the electrical behaviour of NMOS and PMOS transistors through characteristic analysis.

The analysis includes:

- Transfer characteristics.
- Drain characteristics.
- Relationship between gate voltage and drain current.
- MOSFET operating regions.
- Transistor behaviour under different biasing conditions.

### CMOS Digital Circuit Design

Worked on transistor-level implementations of digital logic structures, focusing on how complementary MOS transistors are interconnected to achieve the required logic functionality.

The design work involves circuit schematic development, transistor-level connectivity, logic functionality, simulation-based observation, and testbench preparation where applicable.

### CMOS Memory Structures

Explored CMOS-based memory circuit structures, including SRAM and DRAM.

The work focuses on:

- Basic memory cell architecture.
- Storage of binary information.
- Transistor-based memory structures.
- Differences between SRAM and DRAM.
- The role of memory cells in digital systems.

SRAM and DRAM designs are documented at the schematic level. Testbench-based simulation verification is not claimed for these designs.

## 5. Design and Simulation Workflow

The general workflow followed during the project:

1. Circuit Understanding: Studied the required circuit functionality and underlying CMOS concepts.
2. Schematic Development: Created transistor-level circuit schematics using Tanner S-Edit.
3. Circuit Configuration: Established the required transistor connections and circuit parameters.
4. Testbench Preparation: Prepared testbenches for applicable circuit designs.
5. Simulation: Used T-Spice to perform circuit simulations.
6. Waveform Analysis: Observed simulation outputs and analysed circuit behaviour.
7. Documentation: Organised available schematics, testbenches, and simulation results in this repository.
